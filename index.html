01 / THE BEGINNING

EVERY STORY STARTS SOMEWHERE

From a first step
to fashion history.

Bella Hadid was born in 1996 in Washington, D.C. She grew up around horses and had an interest in photography before fashion became her full-time world.

Her professional modeling story began in 2014, when she signed with IMG Models. That same year, she made her New York Fashion Week debut walking for Desigual. One first step, and a whole new chapter opened.
